# الموسم التالت: «ليلة التماثيل» (user decision 2026-11-10)

- Inspired only by the general "statues come alive at night" idea (common to many films). Do NOT copy any film's characters, scenes, names or jokes; no statues of real famous people — only archetypes.
- Hook from S2 finale: صفيح's screen: «أنا راجع» — رشدي.
- The secret: رشدي (inventor) had a hidden wax-figure workshop on the closed top floor. Every night at 12 the figures move — they are old animatronic robots built by رشدي (same tech as صفيح). Logical explanation at the end (brand rule: no real ghosts).
- Figures (archetypes): فرعون مغرور، مملوك بسيفه، فتوة شعبي، مطربة قديمة، أبو الهول صغير بيتكلم.
- Comedy: شطة wants to befriend the Pharaoh; زلطة computes escape odds from the Mamluk; توتة figures out the timer.
- Thrill: someone wants to steal and sell the figures; the kids must protect them before dawn.
- 10 episodes, same format (hook → events → puzzle → cliffhanger). Plan the episode table after S2 ends.
- **Opening of Season 3 (user, 2026-11-12):** شطة وزلطة بيلعبوا كورة في الشارع → الكورة بتروح البيت اللي فيه التماثيل → الموسم بيبدأ من هنا (the kids must go in to get the ball back). Connects to the S2 finale tease (Roshdy's photo with a covered pharaoh figure + the key tagged «الدور الأخير»).
- **Where (user decision 2026-11-12):** the statues stay INSIDE «عمارة نعناعة» (the series is the building) — the closed «الدور الأخير» floor = Roshdy's wax-figure workshop; the key from the S2E9 box opens it. The S3 opening football must end up in that floor (e.g. the ball flies up onto its closed balcony / through its broken window) → the kids have to go up and open it. S2E10 cliff = the pharaoh's shadow in the top-floor window of the building.
