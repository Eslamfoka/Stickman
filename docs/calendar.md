# Content buffer & daily publishing plan (user, 2026-11-10)

Target before launch: **one full month ready** (30 days × at least 1 post/day, most days 2–3).

## Weekly publishing rhythm (once launched)
| Day | Long (YouTube 16:9 + FB) | Short (TikTok / Reels / Shorts 9:16) |
|---|---|---|
| Sun–Thu | عمارة نعناعة episode | يوميات شطة وزلطة + vertical cut of the episode |
| Fri | لو...؟ | يوميات شطة وزلطة |
| Sat | زلطة بيشرح | ترند الأسبوع |

## Season movie (user, 2026-11-10)
Daily we publish the SHORT episodes (2–3.5 min). When a season's 10 episodes are out, publish the whole season as one **movie** (compiled with `movie/` tools: hook + character intro + all episodes, puzzles kept, recap cards) on YouTube + Facebook — long watch time and mid-roll ads (YouTube needs 8+ min). Zero extra animation cost.
